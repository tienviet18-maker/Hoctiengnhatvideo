In a row house in Edo lived a man named Hachigorō, who failed at everything he tried. ‖ 長屋: row house (Edo tenement). 〜という: named ~. うまくいかない: not go well.
He couldn't keep a job, had no money, and on top of that was buried in debt. ‖ 続かない: not last. おまけに: on top of that. 〜だらけ: full of ~.
You didn't bring any money home again today? ‖ お前さん: you (wife to husband). 〜のかい: (casual question).
What can I do? Wherever I go, nobody will lend me anything. ‖ しょうがねえ = しょうがない: can't be helped (Edo speech). 〜なんか: (dismissive) the likes of.
If we can't buy rice by tomorrow, we can't stay in this house anymore. ‖ 〜までに: by ~. 〜たら: if ~. いられない: can't stay.
Shut up. I'll just get out, then. I'll get out! ‖ うるせえ = うるさい: noisy, shut up. 〜ばいいんだろう: so I just have to ~, right?
Hachigorō slammed the door and stormed outside. ‖ ぴしゃりと: with a slam. 戸: door. 飛び出す: rush out.
Walking through the night streets, Hachigorō did nothing but sigh. ‖ 〜ながら: while ~. ため息をつく: sigh. 〜ばかり: nothing but ~.
Ah, I'm sick of it. Even if I live, nothing good will ever happen. ‖ いやだ: I hate it. 〜ても: even if ~. 一つも〜ない: not a single ~.
Maybe I should just go and die. ‖ いっそのこと: might as well. 死んじまう = 死んでしまう: just die. 〜(よ)うかなあ: maybe I'll ~.
Just then, a low voice came from the darkness behind him. ‖ 暗がり: darkness. 低い: low.
Heh heh heh. You want to die, do you? ‖ 〜のかい: do you?
He turned around to see a skinny old man, nothing but skin and bones. ‖ ふり返る: turn around. 骨と皮ばかり: skin and bones.
His eyes flickered like the flame of a candle. ‖ ろうそく: candle. ちらちら: flickering.
Wh-who are you? ‖ お前: you (rough).
I am a Shinigami, a god of death. ‖ わし: I (old man). 〜さ: (light assertion).
A g-god of death? No way. I don't want to die yet. ‖ 冗談じゃねえ: no way, you must be joking. 〜たくねえ = 〜たくない.
Oh? Then who was it that just said he wanted to die? ‖ おや: oh? さっき: a moment ago.
That was, um, just a slip of the tongue. ‖ 口がすべる: slip of the tongue. 〜だけ: just.
Don't worry. Your lifespan isn't used up yet. Today I've brought you a nice offer. ‖ 寿命: lifespan. 残る: remain. いい話: a good deal.
The Shinigami grinned and brought his face close to Hachigorō's ear. ‖ にやりと: with a grin. 近づける: bring close.
How would you like to become a doctor? ‖ 医者: doctor. 〜気はないか: wouldn't you like to ~.
A doctor? I don't even know the name of a single medicine. ‖ 〜だって: ~, you say? 一つ: even one.
You don't need to. I'll teach you a secret. ‖ 〜なくていい: no need to ~. 〜てやろう: I'll ~ for you.
By every sick person's side sits a Shinigami. Others can't see us, but from now on, I'll make it so only you can. ‖ かならず: always. 〜ようにしてやる: make it so that ~.
If the Shinigami is sitting at the sick person's feet, that person will be saved. ‖ 〜のほう: the side of ~. 助かる: be saved.
When that happens, clap your hands twice and chant this. ‖ たたく: clap. 唱える: chant.
"Ajarakamokuren, tekerettsu no pā." ‖ (a nonsense magic spell)
Ajaraka… what? ‖ 何だって: what was that?
Ajarakamokuren, tekerettsu no pā. Do that, and the Shinigami will go home. ‖ そうすれば: if you do that.
But if the Shinigami is sitting by the pillow, it's hopeless. Never interfere then. ‖ 枕: pillow. 決して〜てはいけない: must never ~.
With that, the Shinigami's figure faded away into the darkness. ‖ すうっと: smoothly, silently. 闇: darkness.
Was it a dream? Well, even if it was, why not give it a try? ‖ 〜でもいい: even ~ is fine. ためしに: as a test.
Hachigorō put up a sheet of paper in front of his house that said "Doctor". ‖ はる: stick, post.
And right away, a messenger came from a big shop nearby. ‖ さっそく: right away. 使いの者: messenger.
Doctor, my master's fever hasn't gone down for days. Please, take a look at him. ‖ 旦那様: master. 熱が下がる: fever goes down. 診る: examine (medically).
When Hachigorō got to the shop, there was a skinny Shinigami sitting neatly at the master's feet. ‖ 足もと: at one's feet. ちょこんと: (sitting) small and neat.
Bingo. It's the feet! ‖ しめた: got it! (lucky)
Hachigorō, putting on airs, peered into the sick man's face. ‖ もったいぶる: put on airs. のぞきこむ: peer into.
Very well, I shall cure him. Everyone, please step back a little. ‖ しんぜよう: I shall ~ (old, pompous). 下がる: step back.
Then he clapped his hands twice, clap clap. ‖ ぱんぱん: clap clap.
Ajarakamokuren, tekerettsu no pā! ‖ (spell)
At that, the Shinigami hopped to his feet and left the room. ‖ ぴょんと: with a hop. 立ち上がる: stand up.
The moment he did, the master's eyes popped open. ‖ とたん: the moment. ぱちり: (eyes) pop open.
Ah, somehow my body feels light all of a sudden. I'm hungry. ‖ なんだか: somehow. 急に: suddenly. 軽い: light.
Master! What a brilliant doctor! ‖ 名医: great doctor.
The shop's people were overjoyed and gave Hachigorō a heap of money as thanks. ‖ 大喜び: great joy. お礼: thanks, reward. どっさり: a heap.
The rumor spread all over Edo in no time. ‖ うわさ: rumor. あっという間: in an instant. 〜中: all over ~.
"They say if that doctor examines you, any illness will be cured." ‖ 〜てもらえば: if you have someone ~. 〜そうだ: I hear.
Every day, a long line formed in front of Hachigorō's house. ‖ 行列ができる: a line forms. 〜ようになる: come to ~.
If the Shinigami was at the feet, he just chanted the spell. If at the pillow, he just said "It's his time" and went home. ‖ 〜だけ: only. 寿命: lifespan, one's time.
Since he was never once wrong, people trusted Hachigorō more and more. ‖ はずれる: miss, be wrong. ますます: more and more. 信じる: believe.
You're amazing! It's the first time in my life I've seen so much money. ‖ 生まれて初めて: for the first time in one's life.
Heh. See? I can do it when I try. ‖ やればできる: can do it if one tries.
He left the old row house, lived in a big home, wore fine kimono, and feasted every night. ‖ 着物: kimono. ごちそう: feast.
He completely forgot about the days when he was poor. ‖ 貧乏: poor. すっかり: completely. 〜てしまう: (completely) end up.
But an easy life never lasts forever. ‖ 楽な暮らし: easy life. 〜ものではない: by nature isn't ~.
His promise with the Shinigami would change Hachigorō's fate greatly, but that's a story for next time. ‖ 約束: promise. 運命: fate. 次のお話: next story.
