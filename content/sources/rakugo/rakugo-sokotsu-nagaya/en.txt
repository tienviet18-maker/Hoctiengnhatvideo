"Sokotsu" means being very careless and jumping to conclusions. ‖ そそっかしい: careless, hasty. 早とちり: jumping to conclusions. 〜というのは〜ことです: ~ means ~.
Today's story is about two scatterbrains living in a nagaya row house in Edo. ‖ 長屋: row house (shared-wall housing of Edo commoners). 粗忽者: scatterbrain.
Hachigorō and Kumagorō were good friends living in the same row house. ‖ 仲のいい: close, on good terms. 同じ: same.
Both had a habit of not listening to people to the end and quickly believing whatever they assumed. ‖ 〜ずに: without ~. 思いこむ: be convinced, assume. 性格: personality.
One morning, Hachigorō went to pray at the Kannon temple in Asakusa. ‖ 観音様: Kannon, goddess of mercy (Sensō-ji). お参り: a visit to pray.
On the way back, a crowd had gathered in front of the temple gate. ‖ 帰り道: the way back. 門: gate. 集まる: gather.
Oh, what's this? Excuse me, let me through to the front. ‖ 〜させてください: please let me ~ (causative + request).
Pushing through to the very front, he saw a man lying on the ground, covered with a cloth. ‖ 〜と: when ~ (discovery). かけられて: covered (passive). 横になる: lie down.
Hey, don't come close without permission. This man collapsed and died here last night. ‖ 勝手に: without permission. 近寄るな: don't come near. 行き倒れる: collapse and die by the road.
An "ikidaore" is someone who collapses on the road and dies right there. ‖ そのまま: just like that. 〜てしまった: ended up ~.
We don't know who he is or where he's from yet. Isn't it a face you know? ‖ どこの誰だか: who and from where. 〜じゃないか: isn't it ~?
Hachigorō lifted the cloth and stared at the man's face. ‖ めくる: lift, turn over. じっと: intently.
Ah! It's Kuma! Kumagorō, who lives in my row house! ‖ あっ: ah! 住んでいる: lives.
I see, you know him. That's a big help. ‖ 知り合い: acquaintance. 助かる: be a help.
Poor guy. When I saw him this morning, he wasn't looking well either. ‖ かわいそうに: poor thing. 元気がない: not well, low.
Wait, wait. This man's been lying here since last night. You can't have seen him this morning. ‖ 〜はずがない: there's no way ~. 〜んだぞ: emphasis.
No, I really did see him. He himself said he felt unwell. ‖ たしかに: definitely. 本人: the person himself. ぐあいが悪い: feel unwell.
He himself? Then this man isn't Kumagorō, is he? ‖ それなら: then. 〜じゃないだろう: surely not ~.
No, Kuma's so careless he died without noticing and went home. ‖ 〜のに: even though ~. 気がつかない: not notice. 〜ないで: without ~.
The official was so dumbfounded he couldn't say a word. ‖ あきれる: be dumbfounded. 言えなくなる: become unable to say.
Right, I'll bring the man himself. I'll have him collect the body. ‖ 連れてくる: bring along. 引き取らせる: have (someone) collect (causative).
The man himself? Hey, wait a minute! ‖ おい: hey. ちょっと待て: wait (command).
Without listening to the official, Hachigorō ran back to the row house. ‖ 〜ずに: without ~. 走って帰る: run back.
At the row house, Kumagorō was calmly eating breakfast. ‖ のんきに: carefree, calmly.
Hey, Kuma! This is no time to be eating! ‖ 〜なんか: the likes of ~. 〜ている場合じゃない: this is no time to ~.
Oh, it's you, Hachi. What's got you so flustered this early? ‖ あわてる: be flustered. どうしたんだい: what's the matter?
Calm down and listen. You died in Asakusa last night. ‖ 落ち着く: calm down. 〜んだ: explanation, assertion.
Huh, me? Dead? ‖ えっ: huh?
That's right. You collapsed in front of the temple gate, and you're still lying there now. ‖ 倒れる: collapse. 今も: even now.
But I don't feel like I died, though. ‖ 〜ような気がしない: don't feel as if ~. 〜けど: but.
That's exactly how careless you are. You die and don't even notice. ‖ 〜ところ: trait, side. 〜ても: even if ~.
Do you remember where you were last night and what you did? ‖ 覚えている: remember.
Last night… I was drinking, and I have no idea how I got home. ‖ どうやって: how. まったく〜ない: not at all.
See? You must have died on your way home. ‖ ほらみろ: see? やっぱり: just as I thought.
Now that you say so, I'm starting to feel that way too. I did feel sluggish this morning… ‖ そう言われると: when you put it that way. そんな気がしてくる: start to feel so. だるい: sluggish.
Right? Come on, let's go collect your body. ‖ 引き取りに行く: go to collect.
I collect my own body myself? Somehow I'm not keen on it. ‖ なんだか: somehow. 気が進まない: not feel like it.
Who else would go? It's your body. ‖ ほかに: else. 〜なんだから: since it's ~.
And so the two hurried to Asakusa. ‖ こうして: and so. 急ぐ: hurry.
You again? …So who's the man behind you? ‖ また: again. 後ろ: behind.
This is Kumagorō himself. He's come to collect his own body. ‖ 本人の: ~ himself. 〜に来ました: came to ~.
What are you talking about? There's no way a living man can collect his own body. ‖ 〜わけがない: there's no way ~. 生きている: living.
But Kumagorō was no longer listening to the official. ‖ しかし: but. もう〜ていない: no longer ~.
Kumagorō lifted the cloth and peered closely at the dead man's face. ‖ のぞきこむ: peer into.
…Ah, it's true. This is me. ‖ 本当だ: it's true.
What a pitiful face I've got. Last night's sake was a bad idea. ‖ なんて〜: what a ~. 情けない: pitiful. 〜んだなあ: exclamation.
Weeping, Kumagorō lifted the dead man up in his arms. ‖ 〜ながら: while ~. 抱き起こす: lift up in one's arms.
Oh me, I'm sorry. You must have been cold, all alone in a place like this. ‖ すまなかった: I'm sorry. 〜ただろう: must have ~.
Come on, Kuma, let's take him home quickly. ‖ 連れて帰る: take home.
Just then, Kumagorō suddenly tilted his head. ‖ ふと: suddenly. 首をかしげる: tilt one's head (puzzled).
Hey, Hachi. There's just one thing I don't get. ‖ 〜だけ: only. 〜んだ: explanation.
What is it? ‖ 何だい: what is it?
The one being held is definitely me. But then who on earth is the me holding him? ‖ 抱かれている: being held (passive). いったい: on earth. 〜なんだろう: I wonder.
Believing others even about who you are. That's the "ochi" of the rakugo classic "Sokotsu Nagaya". ‖ 〜まで: even ~. 信じてしまう: end up believing. 名作: masterpiece.
It's a funny story, but believing what people say without checking may be a scary thing. ‖ 確かめる: check, confirm. 〜ずに: without ~. 〜かもしれません: may be.
