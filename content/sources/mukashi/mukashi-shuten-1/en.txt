Long ago, a frightening rumor spread through the capital, Kyoto. ‖ うわさ: rumor. 広がる: spread.
It was said that when night fell, young women disappeared one after another. ‖ 次々に: one after another. 姿を消す: disappear. 〜というのです: it is said that.
One night, the daughter of a high counselor also vanished without anyone noticing. ‖ 気づかれずに: without being noticed. 〜てしまう: ended up ~.
Someone, anyone, find my daughter! Give my daughter back! ‖ 〜てくれ: (please) do ~ for me.
The counselor summoned Abe no Seimei, the finest diviner in the capital. ‖ 占い師: diviner. 呼ぶ: summon.
Seimei quietly closed his eyes and divined for a while. ‖ 閉じる: close. 占う: divine.
The one who took the princess is not human. ‖ 連れ去る: carry off. 〜のは: the one who ~.
It is the work of an oni called Shuten-dōji, who lives on Mount Ōe in Tanba. ‖ 仕業: deed (usually bad). 住む: live.
Drinking the sake he loves, the oni snatches people from the capital every night. ‖ 〜ながら: while ~. さらう: snatch, kidnap.
Hearing this, the Emperor at once summoned a warrior. ‖ 武士: warrior, samurai. すぐに: at once.
It was Minamoto no Raikō, known as a master archer. ‖ 名人: master. 〜として知られる: known as ~.
Raikō, defeat the oni of Mount Ōe and save the people of the capital. ‖ 退治する: exterminate. 助ける: save.
As you command. Even at the cost of my life, I will surely bring the young women home. ‖ かしこまりました: as you command. 命にかえても: even at the cost of my life.
Raikō had four strong retainers, known as the "Four Heavenly Kings". ‖ 家来: retainer. 〜と呼ばれる: be called ~.
The strongest of them was Sakata no Kintoki, who as a child had wrestled bears in the mountains. ‖ 力持ち: strongman. すもうを取る: wrestle (sumo).
Oni or whatever, I'll crush them with these hands! ‖ 〜でも何でも: ~ or whatever. ひねりつぶす: twist and crush.
Wait, Kintoki. We face hundreds of oni. We can't win with strength alone. ‖ 相手: opponent. 〜だけでは: with ~ alone.
These words came from Watanabe no Tsuna, the calmest of the Four Heavenly Kings. ‖ 冷静: calm, cool-headed.
Tsuna is right. If we fight head-on, we'll be the ones defeated. ‖ 〜とおり: just as ~. 正面から: head-on. やられる: be beaten.
We will disguise ourselves as yamabushi, monks who train in the mountains, and slip into the oni's castle. ‖ 修行: ascetic training. 化ける: disguise oneself. 入りこむ: slip in. 〜(よ)う: let's ~.
The five put on yamabushi robes, hid their swords in their back-chests, and left the capital. ‖ 刀: sword. 笈: monk's back-chest. 隠す: hide.
Before heading to Mount Ōe, the group prayed to the gods at a shrine. ‖ 一行: party, group. 祈る: pray.
Please grant us the strength to defeat the oni. ‖ お与えください: please grant (honorific).
When they reached the foot of the mountain, three white-bearded old men stood by the river. ‖ 入り口: entrance, foot. ひげ: beard.
Lord Raikō, we have been waiting for you. ‖ 殿: lord. お待ちしておりました: we've been waiting (humble).
How do you know my name? ‖ なぜ: why, how.
We are the gods of the shrine where you prayed. We have come to lend you our strength. ‖ わしら: we (old men). 〜じゃ: = だ (elderly). 力を貸す: lend strength.
The old men handed Raikō a jar of sake. ‖ 渡す: hand over.
This is a mysterious sake called "Jinben Kidoku". ‖ 不思議: mysterious.
If a human drinks it, strength wells up; if an oni drinks it, its body goes numb and it cannot move. ‖ 〜ば: if ~. わく: well up. しびれる: go numb.
Make the oni drink it, and strike when they fall asleep. ‖ 飲ませる: make drink (causative). 〜たところを: at the moment ~. 〜がよい: you should ~.
The old men also gave Raikō a helmet decorated with stars. ‖ 兜: helmet. くれる: give (to me/us).
As long as you wear this, no oni's fangs will reach your head. ‖ 〜ていれば: as long as ~. 牙: fang. 届かん: = 届かない.
Raikō bowed deeply, and when he raised his face, the old men were nowhere to be seen. ‖ 頭を下げる: bow. 顔を上げる: look up.
Th-they vanished! They really were gods! ‖ 消える: vanish. 〜んだ: so it was ~.
The group spent many days climbing the steep mountain path. ‖ けわしい: steep. 〜をかけて: spending (time).
The valleys were deep, the fog thick, and the mountain dark as night even at midday. ‖ 谷: valley. 霧: fog. 濃い: thick, dense.
One day, by the riverbank, a young woman was crying as she washed clothes. ‖ ほとり: bank. 洗う: wash.
Excuse me, what are you doing so deep in the mountains? ‖ もし: excuse me (old-fashioned). 山奥: deep in the mountains.
I was carried off from the capital by the oni. ‖ さらわれる: be kidnapped.
Every day I am made to wash the oni's clothes. ‖ 洗わされる: be made to wash (causative-passive).
The counselor's daughter and the other young women are all locked up in the oni's castle. ‖ 閉じこめる: lock up.
Don't worry. We have come to rescue you all. ‖ 安心: relief, peace of mind. 〜に来る: come to ~.
Really? But the oni are very strong, and there are hundreds of them. ‖ しかも: moreover.
There's no need to worry. We have strength given to us by the gods. ‖ 心配はいらない: no need to worry. いただく: receive (humble).
In that case, I'll show you the way to the oni's castle. ‖ それなら: in that case. お教えします: I'll show you (humble).
If you follow this river upstream, you'll find a great iron gate. ‖ さかのぼる: go upstream. 〜と: when ~.
Shuten-dōji loves sake, and every night he gathers his underlings and holds a feast. ‖ 手下: underling. 宴: feast.
I see. That works in our favor. ‖ なるほど: I see. 〜にとって: for ~. 都合がいい: convenient.
Just as the woman told them, the group followed the river upstream. ‖ 教えられたとおり: as they were told.
After a while, a huge black gate came into view beyond the fog. ‖ 向こう: beyond. 見えてくる: come into view.
In front of the gate stood two huge horned oni, glaring at them. ‖ 角: horn. 生える: grow. にらむ: glare.
Hey, you yamabushi lot. Where do you think you are? ‖ 〜ども: you lot (contemptuous).
We are traveling yamabushi who have lost our way. Could you let us stay just one night? ‖ 迷う: get lost. 泊めていただけませんか: could you let us stay.
Hmph, humans coming up here, that's rare. I'll go ask the boss, so wait there. ‖ 〜とは: (surprise) that ~. 親分: boss. 〜ていろ: (command) stay ~.
When the oni disappeared inside the gate, Raikō looked at each of the Four Heavenly Kings in turn. ‖ 〜ずつ: one by one.
This is it. No one must reveal who we really are. ‖ いよいよ: at last, now. 正体: true identity. 〜てはならない: must not ~.
The heavy iron gate creaked and slowly swung open. ‖ ギギギ: creak. 音を立てる: make a sound.
What awaits them inside the oni's castle? The story continues next time. ‖ 〜でしょうか: I wonder ~. 次回: next time. 続く: continue.
