Long ago, near a certain mountain, there lived a man known as the old bamboo cutter. ‖ 竹取: bamboo cutting. 〜と呼ばれる: be called ~.
Every day the old man went to the mountain, cut bamboo, and made baskets and sieves to sell. ‖ 竹: bamboo. かご: basket. ざる: sieve, colander.
He lived with his wife, poor but peaceful. ‖ 貧しい: poor. 〜けれど: but. 暮らす: live.
One day, when the old man went into the bamboo grove as usual, something strange happened. ‖ いつものように: as usual. 不思議: mysterious. 起こる: happen.
The base of one bamboo stalk was glowing softly. ‖ 根元: base, root. ぼうっと: dimly, softly.
Oh? Why is that bamboo glowing? ‖ おや: oh? どうして: why.
The old man came closer and gently cut that bamboo open. ‖ そっと: gently. 〜てみる: try ~.
And inside the bamboo, a tiny girl small enough to sit on his palm was sitting there neatly. ‖ 〜ほど: so ~ that. 手のひら: palm. ちょこんと: neatly, primly.
What an adorable child. Surely the gods have given her to us. ‖ かわいらしい: adorable. 〜にちがいない: surely ~.
The old man wrapped the girl carefully in both hands and took her home. ‖ 両手: both hands. 包む: wrap. 連れて帰る: bring home.
Oh my, dear, where did that child come from? ‖ どうしたんですか: what happened?, where's that from?
She was inside a bamboo stalk. From today, let's raise her as our own child. ‖ わしら: we (old man's speech). 〜として: as ~. 育てる: raise.
She's so tiny and so sweet. Let's raise her with great care. ‖ 大切に: with care.
The two put the girl in a little basket and raised her with the greatest care. ‖ それはそれは: ever so, truly.
From that day on, whenever the old man cut bamboo, strange things kept happening. ‖ 続く: continue. 〜ようになる: come to ~.
Gold came pouring out from between the joints of the bamboo. ‖ 節: node, joint. 金: gold. ざくざく: in heaps.
Look, dear. Gold came out of the bamboo again. ‖ 〜ておくれ: please ~ (old-fashioned).
My goodness. Ever since she came to us, it's been nothing but good luck. ‖ 〜てから: since ~. 〜ばかり: nothing but ~.
The old man's household gradually grew rich. ‖ だんだん: gradually. お金持ち: rich person.
The girl, too, was unlike ordinary children and grew up remarkably fast. ‖ 〜とはちがって: unlike ~. ぐんぐん: rapidly.
In just three months, she was already as tall as a grown woman. ‖ たった: only, just. 〜か月: ~ months.
And she became a young woman of beauty beyond compare. ‖ たとえようもない: beyond comparison. 娘: daughter, young woman.
With her in it, the house glowed bright even on dark nights. ‖ 明るい: bright.
When she's here, we forget all our sorrows and hardships, don't we? ‖ つらい: hard, painful. 忘れてしまう: forget completely.
The old man called a wise man from the village and had him give the girl a name. ‖ 物知り: knowledgeable person. 名前をつける: give a name. 〜てもらう: have someone ~.
She is so beautiful she seems to shine, so let us name her "Nayotake no Kaguya-hime." ‖ 〜ように: as if ~. 名づける: name. なよ竹: slender bamboo. かぐや: radiant.
And so the girl came to be called Princess Kaguya. ‖ 〜ようになる: come to ~.
Rumors of Princess Kaguya spread throughout the capital in no time. ‖ うわさ: rumor. 〜じゅう: throughout ~. 広がる: spread.
They say there's a princess of rare beauty at the bamboo cutter's house. ‖ 世にもまれな: rare in this world. 〜そうだ: I hear ~.
I'd love to meet her, even if just for a glance. ‖ ひと目: one glance. 〜でいいから: even just ~. 〜たいものだ: I'd really like to ~.
Every day, many men gathered around the old man's house. ‖ 集まる: gather.
They peeked through gaps in the hedge, and some stood in front of the house all night without sleeping. ‖ 垣根: hedge, fence. すき間: gap. のぞく: peek. 〜たり〜たり: doing things like ~ and ~.
But Princess Kaguya would not meet anyone. ‖ 〜(よ)うとしない: refuse to ~.
After many months, most of the men gave up and went home. ‖ ほとんど: most. あきらめる: give up.
Even so, five men remained who would not give up to the very end. ‖ それでも: even so. 最後まで: to the end. 残る: remain.
All five were splendid nobles of high rank. ‖ 身分: social rank. 貴族: noble.
Through rain and snow, the five came to the old man's house almost every day. ‖ 〜のように: almost like ~. やって来る: come along.
Kaguya, my dear. I'm an old man past seventy now. ‖ 〜を過ぎる: be past ~. 年寄り: old person.
When I die, you'll be left all alone. ‖ ひとりぼっち: all alone. 〜てしまう: (sadly) end up ~.
Won't you marry one of those five men? ‖ 〜てくれないか: won't you ~ for me?
Grandfather, I don't want to marry yet. ‖ まだ〜たくない: don't want to ~ yet.
But if you feel that strongly, I will think about it. ‖ おっしゃる: say (honorific). 〜なら: if ~.
I will marry whoever brings me the treasure I name. ‖ 宝物: treasure. 方: person (polite). 〜ことにする: decide to ~.
A treasure, eh? Very well, I understand. I'll go and tell them. ‖ 伝える: convey. 〜てくる: go and ~.
The old man invited the five nobles inside and conveyed Princess Kaguya's words. ‖ 言葉: words.
A treasure, you say? We'll bring anything at all! ‖ 〜ですと？: ~, you say? 何でも: anything.
From behind the bamboo blind, Princess Kaguya quietly named a treasure for each man in turn. ‖ すだれ: bamboo blind. 奥: inner part. 〜ずつ: each, one at a time.
For Prince Ishitsukuri: the shining stone begging bowl said to have been used by the Buddha. ‖ 皇子: prince. 仏様: the Buddha. 〜という: said to be ~. おはち: bowl.
For Prince Kuramochi: a branch from a tree on Mount Hōrai in the eastern sea, with roots of silver, a trunk of gold, and fruits of white jewels. ‖ 蓬莱: Mount Hōrai (mythical isle). 銀: silver. 根: root. くき: stem. 玉: jewel. 実: fruit. 枝: branch.
For the Minister of the Right: a robe made from the hide of the fire-rat, which never burns. ‖ 右大臣: Minister of the Right. 燃える: burn. 火ねずみ: fire-rat (legendary). 皮: hide, skin.
For the Great Counselor: the five-colored jewel that shines on a dragon's neck. ‖ 大納言: Great Counselor. 竜: dragon. 首: neck. 五色: five colors.
For the Middle Counselor: the cowrie shell that swallows are said to keep. ‖ 中納言: Middle Counselor. つばめ: swallow. 子安貝: cowrie shell (charm for safe childbirth).
Hearing this, the five looked at one another. ‖ 顔を見合わせる: exchange glances.
Do such things really exist in this world? ‖ この世: this world. 〜だろうか: I wonder if ~.
Every one of them was a legendary treasure that no one had ever seen. ‖ 伝説: legend. 〜たことがない: have never ~. 〜ばかり: all, nothing but.
But wanting with all their hearts to marry Princess Kaguya, the five nodded. ‖ 一心で: single-mindedly. うなずく: nod.
Will the five men really be able to find the treasures? ‖ 〜ことができる: be able to ~.
Next time, we'll tell the funny and slightly sad ending of the five men's treasure hunts. ‖ 探す: search. おかしい: funny. 結末: outcome, ending.
