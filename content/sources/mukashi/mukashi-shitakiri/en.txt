One winter morning, the old man found a small, injured sparrow in the snow. ‖ けがをする: get hurt. 雀: sparrow. 見つける: find.

One day, after the old man went to work on the mountain, the old woman made starch paste for the laundry. ‖ 洗濯: laundry. のり: starch paste.
But the moment she looked away, half the paste was gone. ‖ 目をはなす: look away. すき: unguarded moment.

Crying, the sparrow flew off to a distant bamboo grove. ‖ 竹やぶ: bamboo grove.
In the evening, the old man came home from the mountain and was shocked to find the sparrow gone. ‖ 驚く: be surprised.
