The next morning, Anju made a request of Sanshō Dayū. ‖ お願い: request.
Please let me go to the mountain with my brother to cut brushwood too. ‖ 〜させてください: please let me ~. 柴を刈る: cut brushwood.
Dayū laughed and said, "Do as you like." ‖ 好きにしろ: do as you please.
When they reached the top of the mountain, Anju took her brother's hand and quietly began to speak. ‖ 手を取る: take someone's hand. 〜はじめる: begin to ~.
Zushiō, listen carefully. Today you must run away from here. ‖ 逃げる: run away. 〜なさい: you must ~.
Run away? Not unless you come too, sister. ‖ 〜でなければ: unless ~. いや: I don't want to.
If we both run, they'll find us right away. ‖ 見つかる: be found.
Rather than suffer here your whole life, run, even alone, and look for Father and Mother. ‖ 一生: whole life. 〜くらいなら: rather than ~. 探す: look for.
Anju took the amulet from her neck and hung it around her brother's. ‖ はずす: take off. 首にかける: hang around the neck.
Jizō will surely protect you. ‖ 守ってくださる: (he) will protect.
When you come down the mountain, follow the river. There should be a temple. ‖ 〜に沿って: along ~. 〜はず: should be.
What about you, sister? ‖ どうするの: what will you do.
Don't worry about me. I'll come after you, I promise. ‖ 心配しないで: don't worry. あとから: later, after.
Zushiō went down the mountain, looking back again and again. ‖ ふり返る: look back. 〜ながら: while ~.
Anju watched him go until he was out of sight. ‖ 姿: figure. 見送る: see off. 〜まで: until.
Then Anju walked alone, deep into the mountain, to the marsh. ‖ 奥: depths. 沼: marsh.
It is said that on the edge of the marsh, only Anju's straw sandals were left, neatly placed side by side. ‖ そば: beside. わらぞうり: straw sandals. きちんと: neatly. 〜そうです: it is said.
To let her brother escape, Anju had given her own life. ‖ 逃がす: let escape. 命をささげる: give one's life.
Meanwhile, Zushiō kept running along the river and came to a small temple. ‖ 一方: meanwhile. たどり着く: finally reach.
What happened to you, child? ‖ 姿: appearance, state.
Please help me. My pursuers are coming. ‖ 追っ手: pursuers.
Without asking anything, the monk hid Zushiō inside a big chest. ‖ 〜ずに: without ~. 隠す: hide.
Soon, Saburō and his men came to the temple carrying torches. ‖ まもなく: soon. たいまつ: torch.
Did a child run in here? ‖ 〜てこなかったか: didn't ~ come?
This is the Buddha's temple. If you act violently, you will be punished. ‖ 乱暴: violence. 罰が当たる: be punished (by heaven).
The monk was so calm that Saburō and his men had no choice but to give up and go home. ‖ 落ち着く: be calm. あきらめる: give up. 〜ざるをえない: have no choice but to ~.
You're safe now. Come with me to the capital. ‖ 大丈夫: all right. わし: I (old man).
Led by the monk, Zushiō set off for the faraway capital, Kyō. ‖ 連れられる: be led. 向かう: head for.
In the capital, Zushiō prayed through the night at a temple called Kiyomizu-dera. ‖ ひと晩: one night. 祈る: pray.
The next morning, a high-ranking minister had come to the temple to worship. ‖ 身分の高い: high-ranking. 大臣: minister. お参り: visit to worship.
Seeing the amulet around Zushiō's neck, the minister gave a start. ‖ はっとする: be startled.
Where did you get that Jizō amulet? ‖ 手に入れる: obtain.
This amulet has been handed down in my family for generations. ‖ 伝わる: be handed down.
Zushiō told him everything about his father, his mother and his sister. ‖ すべて: everything.
I see. So you are the son of Lord Masauji? ‖ そなた: you (archaic). 殿: Lord.
Everyone knows now that Lord Masauji was only blamed falsely, and was truly a just man. ‖ 〜だけ: only. 本当は: in truth.
The minister took Zushiō into his own house and had him study. ‖ 連れて帰る: take home. 勉強させる: have someone study.
Zushiō studied without sleeping at night and grew into a fine young man. ‖ 〜ないで: without ~. 立派: fine, admirable.
Some years later, to everyone's surprise, Zushiō was chosen as governor of Tango. ‖ なんと: amazingly. 国守: provincial governor. 選ばれる: be chosen.
As soon as he became governor, Zushiō banned the buying and selling of people throughout the province. ‖ 〜とともに: at the same time as ~. 禁じる: forbid.
The people forced to work in Sanshō Dayū's house were all set free. ‖ 働かされる: be made to work. 自由: free.
Jirō happily gave the servants money to go home. ‖ 配る: hand out.
Zushiō went to the mountain marsh and built a small shrine hall for his sister. ‖ お堂: shrine hall. 建てる: build.
Sister, thanks to you, I am alive. ‖ 〜のおかげで: thanks to ~.
How Zushiō longed to see his sister just once more! ‖ どんなに〜ことか: how (very) ~!
Around that time, sad news arrived from Tsukushi. ‖ 知らせ: news. 届く: arrive.
His father Masauji had died long before. ‖ 亡くなる: pass away.
Only his mother in Sado remained. ‖ 残る: remain.
Zushiō crossed the sea to Sado to look for his mother. ‖ 渡る: cross.
But no matter how much he asked around in Sado, he could not find out where she was. ‖ いくら〜ても: no matter how much ~. 行方: whereabouts.
At the end of a long journey, Zushiō came to a field at the edge of a village. ‖ 〜の末に: at the end of ~. 村はずれ: edge of a village.
In the field, a blind old woman was singing as she chased away the birds. ‖ 目の見えない: blind. 鳥を追う: chase birds.
Oh, how I miss Anju, hōyare-ho. Oh, how I miss Zushiō, hōyare-ho. ‖ 恋しや: how I miss (archaic). ほうやれほ: call to scare birds.
The moment he heard that voice, Zushiō's heart leapt. ‖ 〜たとたん: the moment ~. どきん: thump.
Mother! It's me! It's Zushiō! ‖ わたしです: it's me.
Zushiō? It can't be. My children are gone. ‖ うそでしょう: it can't be.
Zushiō took out the amulet and gently touched it to his mother's eyes. ‖ 取り出す: take out. 当てる: put against.
Then, wondrously, his mother's eyes slowly opened. ‖ 不思議: wondrous. 少しずつ: little by little.
Oh, I can see. Zushiō, it really is you. ‖ 見える: can see.
The two held each other tight and cried aloud. ‖ しっかり: tightly. 声をあげて: aloud.
Far across the sea, Anju too must have been smiling. ‖ 〜ことでしょう: must have ~. ほほえむ: smile.
And so the little Jizō amulet protected the family to the very end. ‖ こうして: in this way. 最後まで: to the end.
