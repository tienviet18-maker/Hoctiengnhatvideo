Once upon a time, in a village at the foot of a mountain, there lived an old man and an old woman. ‖ 昔々: once upon a time. ふもと: foot (of a mountain). 住む: to live.
Every day, the old man went to the mountain to gather firewood, and the old woman went to the river to do the washing. ‖ しばかり: gathering brushwood. せんたく: laundry. 〜に行く: go to ~.

One day, while the old woman was washing clothes in the river, something came floating down from upstream. ‖ 〜ていると: while ~ing. 流れてくる: to float this way.
