As summer drew to a close, Princess Kaguya wept at the moon almost every night. ‖ 終わるころ: around when it ends. 毎晩のように: almost every night.
Kaguya, what's wrong? What makes you so sad? ‖ どうしたの: what's wrong. そんなに: so (much).
Kaguya was silent for a while, but at last she wiped her tears and began to speak. ‖ 黙る: be silent. やがて: before long. 涙をふく: wipe tears.
Grandfather, Grandmother. I have hidden it until now, but I will tell you the truth. ‖ 隠す: hide. お話しします: I will tell (humble).
I am not a person of this land. I come from the capital on the moon. ‖ 都: capital. 〜者なのです: I am one who ~ (explaining).
Wh-what? You say you came from the moon? ‖ なんだって: what did you say. 〜というのか: you mean ~?
Yes. For certain reasons, I was sent down to this world for a time. ‖ 事情: circumstances. 下ろされる: be sent down (passive).
On the night of the full moon, the fifteenth day of the eighth month, someone will come from the moon to fetch me. ‖ 満月: full moon. 迎え: escort, someone to fetch.
When that happens, I will have to go back. ‖ 〜なければならない: must ~.
No! After you've grown up so beautifully! ‖ せっかく: after all that effort. 〜のに: even though.
No matter who comes, I will never hand you over. ‖ 絶対に: absolutely. 渡す: hand over.
I don't really want to go back either. I want to stay with you both forever. ‖ 本当は: actually. ずっと: forever.
That is why I have been crying every night, looking at the moon. ‖ 〜ては: (repeatedly) whenever ~.
The old man told the Emperor about this. ‖ 帝: the Emperor. 知らせる: inform.
I will never let Kaguya go to the moon! ‖ 〜てなるものか: I'll never let ~ happen.
The Emperor sent two thousand soldiers to the bamboo cutter's house. ‖ 兵士: soldier. 送る: send.
Soldiers lined up in rows on the roof and all around the fence. ‖ ずらりと: in a long row. 垣根: hedge, fence.
The old woman held Kaguya tight and hid in the innermost room of the house. ‖ しっかり: tightly. 奥: back, innermost part. 隠れる: hide.
The old man locked the door of the room and planted himself in front of it. ‖ かぎをかける: lock. 座りこむ: sit down and stay.
Moon people or whoever, come if you dare! ‖ 〜でも何でも: ~ or whatever. 来るなら来てみろ: come if you dare.
And at last, the night of the fifteenth of the eighth month arrived. ‖ とうとう: at last.
At midnight, the sky suddenly began to shine, brighter than midday. ‖ 真夜中: midnight. 急に: suddenly. 〜よりも: than ~.
Out of the full moon's light, people riding on clouds quietly came down. ‖ 雲に乗る: ride a cloud. 下りる: come down.
They were the heavenly beings from the moon capital. A flying carriage came down with them. ‖ 天人: heavenly being. 空飛ぶ車: flying carriage.
The soldiers tried to draw their bows, but no matter what, they had no strength. ‖ 弓を引く: draw a bow. どうしても〜ない: no matter what, cannot.
As if in a dream, no one was able to move. ‖ まるで〜ように: just as if. 〜てしまう: ended up ~.
Even the door that should have been locked slid open by itself. ‖ 〜はずの: that should have ~. ひとりでに: by itself. すうっと: smoothly.
Princess Kaguya. The promised time has come. Now, let us return to the moon capital. ‖ 約束の時: promised time. さあ: now, come.
Weeping, Kaguya bowed low with her hands on the floor before the old couple. ‖ 〜ながら: while ~. 手をつく: bow with hands on the floor.
It is unbearable to leave without repaying even a little of the kindness of raising me. ‖ 恩: debt of gratitude. 〜まま: while still ~. 〜てたまらない: unbearably ~.
This is the robe I wore. Please treasure it, and think of it as me. ‖ 〜と思って: thinking of it as ~. 大切にする: treasure.
When the moon rises, please look up at the sky. I will be watching you both from the moon. ‖ どうか: please. 見上げる: look up.
Kaguya also wrote a letter to the Emperor and added a small jar the heavenly beings had brought. ‖ つぼ: jar. そえる: attach, add.
In the jar was the elixir of immortality: drink it and you will never die. ‖ 決して〜ない: never. 不死: immortality.
One heavenly being came toward Kaguya, holding a glittering feathered robe. ‖ きらきら: glittering. 羽衣: feathered robe. 近づく: approach.
If you put on this robe, you will forget all the sorrows and pains of this world. ‖ 〜ば: if ~. すべて: all.
Please wait. Before I put it on, let me say just one more word. ‖ 〜る前に: before ~. 言わせてください: please let me say.
Grandfather, Grandmother. Thank you, truly, truly. ‖ 本当に: truly.
When Kaguya put on the feathered robe, the sadness vanished from her face at once. ‖ すっと: smoothly, at once. 消える: vanish.
She could no longer even remember the old couple. ‖ 思い出せる: can recall. 〜なくなる: no longer ~.
Kaguya boarded the flying carriage and rose to the moon with the heavenly beings. ‖ 昇る: ascend. 〜ていく: go off ~.
The old man and woman could only weep as they watched the light grow distant. ‖ 遠くなる: grow distant. ただ: only. 見送る: see off.
Kaguya! Kaguya! ‖ (calling out)
From then on, the two sank so deep into grief that they could not even eat. ‖ 〜ほど: to the extent that. 沈む: sink.
The letter and the elixir were delivered to the Emperor by a messenger. ‖ 使いの者: messenger. 〜によって: by. 届ける: deliver.
The Emperor read the letter and said nothing for a long time. ‖ 長い間: for a long time.
If I can never see Kaguya again, what use is a medicine that keeps me from dying? ‖ 二度と〜ない: never again. 何の役に立とうか: what use is it.
The Emperor asked his retainers which mountain was closest to heaven. ‖ 家来: retainer. たずねる: ask.
They say that in the land of Suruga there is a mountain higher than the clouds. ‖ 駿河: Suruga (today's Shizuoka). 〜そうです: they say.
The Emperor had many soldiers carry the letter and the elixir to the summit of that mountain. ‖ 運ばせる: have someone carry (causative). 頂上: summit.
And he ordered them to burn the letter and the elixir at the very top of the mountain. ‖ 燃やす: burn. 〜ように命じる: order to ~.
The smoke rose straight up into the sky, toward the moon. ‖ 煙: smoke. まっすぐに: straight.
Because it was the mountain where the elixir of immortality (fushi) was burned, it came to be called "Mount Fuji". ‖ 〜ので: because. 〜と呼ばれるようになる: come to be called ~.
It is also said that because so many soldiers climbed it, it came to be written 富士山, "the mountain rich in soldiers". ‖ 士: soldier. 富む: be rich in. 〜とも言われる: it is also said.
People long ago, it is said, remembered that smoke whenever they saw clouds rising above Mount Fuji. ‖ 昇る雲: rising clouds. 〜といいます: it is said.
On a bright moonlit night, try looking up at the sky. Somewhere, Kaguya might be watching. ‖ 〜てみてください: try ~ing. 〜かもしれない: might.
And that is the end of the tale of Princess Kaguya. ‖ おしまい: the end.
